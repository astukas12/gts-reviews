Talladega Trucks: Plate Track Profile

Plate races: wrecks first, then finish among survivors

The short version

Plate races are decided by who is still running. Trucks wreck at about the same rate whoever they are and wherever they start; quality and plate history show up only in where the survivors finish.

01How often trucks wreck, race by race

The wreck count is the variable. Truck plate races ran from 4 to 15 DNFs in a 36-car field (median 9). Cautions and DNFs move together (r = 0.46 in Trucks, 0.61 across all 46 plate races); more lead changes mean fewer wrecks (r = -0.46 in Trucks).

03Does where you start matter?

Barely, in Trucks. DNF rate by start bucket is 19% to 33% with no steady direction, and starting 11th to 30th gives about the same top-10 rate as the front. Deep starters (31st or worse) finish a bit worse (17.3 against 13.7 for survivors starting 1-10). Same shape across all three series, with less noise.

04Finish rates by driver form tier

Tiers are thirds of the field ranked on each driver's general form in that series before the race (nothing from the race itself). Wrecks follow form only a little: the strongest third wrecks 23% and the weakest 28%, a gap that is within noise at about 110 driver-races per tier. Where form shows up is the finish among trucks that survive: 12.7 for the strongest third against 14.9 for the weakest.
