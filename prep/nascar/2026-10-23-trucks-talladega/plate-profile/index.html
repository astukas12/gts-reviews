02How many DNFs to predict

The count has a wide spread, so it should be drawn each sim, not fixed. Recommended for 5689: centre on about 9 of 36 (25% of the field) with a plausible range of 4 to 15. Reasoning: the nine-race median is 9 and mean is 9.3; Talladega alone averages 9.8; the last three races averaged 5.3, which argues for the low end, but nothing about 5689 (85 laps, same 36-car field) differs from the 2024 and 2025 Talladega races (10 and 7). 4 of 9 races had 10 or more DNFs.

03The floor if you don't wreck

Among the 241 truck-finishes that were still running at the end: half finished 14th or better, 75% 21th or better, 90% 26th or better. The last running truck in each race finished between 24 and 33 (average 29.1), so with 9 DNFs the worst a clean truck should expect is about 27 to 28. 83% of runners were on the lead lap. For finish caps (T20, T25, T30): 73% of running trucks finished 20th or better, 88% 25th or better and 98% 30th or better.

04Does where you start matter?

Little. DNF rate by start bucket runs 19% to 33% with no steady direction, and starts 1 to 30 give similar top-10 and top-20 rates. The back of the grid (31st or worse, about 6 trucks a race) is the exception: top 10 falls to 13% and its running trucks have a median finish of 18. Qualifying is not run yet for 5689, so there is no start call for the field.
