How much practice has meant

We have Lap Raptor long-run practice for 6 past races of this series on tracks like this one (Bristol, North Wilkesboro). In those, the long-run order matched the order of race speed at a rank correlation of 0.75 on average (from 0.66 to 0.83). Pre-race strength matched the finish at 0.61 in the same races. Long-run practice is a real signal, about as strong as form but not stronger: lean on it most where the two agree. The samples are small.
