What practice adds

On top of our pre-race form, practice explains about 0.12 of what form missed in the finish (rank partial correlation, 539 cars). Once the grid is known too, that drops to 0.09: practice and qualifying overlap.

Blending practice onto form at weight w (score vs average running position): 0%: 0.781 · 10%: 0.786 · 20%: 0.793 · 30%: 0.797 · 40%: 0.801 · 50%: 0.793.

The same vs finishing position: 0%: 0.580 · 10%: 0.581 · 20%: 0.583 · 30%: 0.576 · 40%: 0.571 · 50%: 0.556.

Read: practice helps most for how a car runs all day, and little for the finish, which wrecks and strategy scramble. Against how a car ran all day the best blend is 30–40% practice; against the finish it is 10–20%. Single-lap speed is the weakest of the three readings for the finish, and a 10-lap window needs a long enough run to exist, so half the field has none.
