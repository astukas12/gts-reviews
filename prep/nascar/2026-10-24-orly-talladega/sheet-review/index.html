From NascarData.xlsx, O'Reilly Daytona and Talladega races 2022 to Aug 2026 (18 races; the dominator pool has 12 of them). Small samples: read as tendencies.

1. Is Talladega different from Daytona for who is good?

  • Partly. Driver average finish at the two tracks correlates only 0.43 (28 drivers with 4+ starts at each); top-10 rate 0.31.
  • Better at Talladega: Jesse Love 12.5 at Daytona to 5.4 (all 5 Talladega starts top 10), Leland Honeyman 19.8 to 9.8, Jeb Burton 17.6 to 11.8, Brennan Poole 18.0 to 12.0, Caesar Bacarella 28.3 to 17.3, Sammy Smith 21.2 to 16.5, Sheldon Creed 14.2 to 11.8.
  • Worse at Talladega: Justin Allgaier 6.6 to 19.9 (38% DNF at Talladega, none at Daytona), Carson Kvapil 6.0 to 17.2, Chandler Smith 10.2 to 23.2, Ryan Sieg 12.9 to 17.6.
  • Teams: RCR 14.2 to 10.5 (best at Talladega, DNF 20% to 12%); JR Motorsports 15.4 to 18.7 (DNF 17% to 33%); Jordan Anderson 15.7 to 19.1 (DNF 20% to 37%); Joe Gibbs 18.9 to 21.2; Alpha Prime 22.5 to 18.8; SS GreenLight 22.3 to 16.7.
  • Read: Talladega rewards RCR and the drivers who have run well there (Love, Creed, Honeyman, Jeb Burton) and punishes the JRM cars more than Daytona does. The sheet does not yet use a per-track split.

2. Has RCR domination tailed off?

  • Finishes: a mild dip. RCR won 4 of the 5 plate races from Feb 2025 to Feb 2026 (Daytona Feb 2025, Talladega Apr and Oct 2025, Daytona Feb 2026; Aug 2025 was a 4th).
