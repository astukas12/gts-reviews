The calls I made

Where I most want your eyes before any testing. Nothing here has been simmed.

  • Biggest issue I see: the weak-ride rules (ported from Trucks) round some priced cars to zero win. Win is under 0.5% on our sheet for: Anthony Alfredo (2.3% market), Jeb Burton (2.3% market). Kalshi has each at 1.5% or more. The top-10 column is also 8-16 points above Kalshi for the top seven cars (ceilings take mass from the mid-pack and hand it up). I have not fixed either; they are yours to call.
  • Wreck rate (placeholder). The plate ladder sim uses a flat 24% of the field out by a wreck; the ride-level DNF risk then scales the upper columns around a 29% field baseline (O'Reilly plate history: 11.2 DNFs a race, 88% crashes). Your number decides how flat the middle of the table is.
  • Top-10 ceiling for strong rides is 56% (JRM, RCR, Haas, Hendrick, Gibbs). That cap is carried over from the Trucks plate rides and was not refit for O'Reilly. Austin Hill's T10 drops from 64% to 56% because of it.
  • No top-5 market and no NASCAR Prob. The base is Kalshi win, top-3 and top-10 only (Kalshi 75% / our ladder 25% on the win column; Kalshi 60-90% on T3 and T10 by ride). T5 is our ladder.
  • Leland Honeyman ($7,100) has no Kalshi price and no usable O'Reilly history here, so he was priced off salary for the market side and landed in the weakest ride tier (T10 ceiling 8%). Say if he deserves better.
  • No hand-picked bottom cars. Friday's list of weak rides was Andrew's. Here weak = shrunk mean running finish of 19th or worse, or fewer than 10 starts of history. Tell me which cars to add or move.
  • Dominators: whole-race lines from the 12-race pool (8 Talladega + 4 Daytona; Aug 2024 Daytona dropped on the shape test, your call to keep). Sheet row 1 carries DomMode = race, which customer main reads.
  • Data caveat: Oct 2022 Talladega shows 0 DNFs in the results database (missing statuses), which pulls the DNF baseline down a little.
