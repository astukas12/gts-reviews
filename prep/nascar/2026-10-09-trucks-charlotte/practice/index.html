How sure is the order

Each long-run gap has a margin of error of about 0.07 seconds a lap. Trucks within about 0.10 s of each other are effectively level, so read the board in tiers rather than as a strict 1-2-3. An asterisk marks a truck whose longest run was under 5 laps: its pace rests on very little.

How much practice has meant

We have Lap Raptor long-run practice for 3 past races of this series on 1.5-mile tracks (Charlotte, Kansas, Homestead-Miami). In those, the long-run order matched the order of race speed at a rank correlation of 0.55 on average (from 0.36 to 0.72); here last time 0.57. Pre-race strength matched the finish at 0.62 in the same races. Long-run practice is a real signal, about as strong as form but not stronger: lean on it most where the two agree. The samples are small.
