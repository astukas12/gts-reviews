How much practice has meant

We have Lap Raptor long-run practice for 20 past races of this series on tracks like this one (Phoenix, New Hampshire, Richmond, Iowa, North Wilkesboro, Bristol, Martinsville). In those, the long-run order matched the order of race speed at a rank correlation of 0.54 on average (from -0.05 to 0.85); here last time 0.62. Pre-race strength matched the finish at 0.50 in the same races. Long-run practice is a real signal, about as strong as form but not stronger: lean on it most where the two agree. The samples are small.
