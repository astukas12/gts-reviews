Correction: the track changed during this session

It sprinkled during the session and the track slowed by about 1.1 seconds a lap. The field ran 0.98 s a lap faster than its own average in the first five minutes and about 0.2 s slower after the 25-minute mark. The lap-window tables on this page (best 5-, 10- and 15-lap averages) are raw, so they favour the drivers who went out early (Crews, Alfredo, Perkins, Gray, Retzlaff, Sawalich) and hurt the late runners (Creed, Day, Mayer, R. Sieg, Hill). The fitted long run is less affected and its order already looks like the corrected one.

How much practice has meant

We have Lap Raptor long-run practice for 6 past races of this series on tracks like this one (Phoenix, Bristol). In those, the long-run order matched the order of race speed at a rank correlation of 0.79 on average (from 0.70 to 0.91); here last time 0.76. Pre-race strength matched the finish at 0.70 in the same races. Long-run practice is a real signal, about as strong as form but not stronger: lean on it most where the two agree. The samples are small.
